Cookie Policy

How Roofly uses browser storage and similar technologies.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser by websites you visit. Similar technologies, such as local storage or session storage, can remember preferences, support interactive features, and help a site work reliably.

2. Types of storage used

Roofly may use essential storage, preference storage, security-related browser data, and technical records created by hosting or delivery services. The exact tools can change as the website is maintained or improved.

3. Essential storage

Essential storage supports core website behavior such as navigation, mobile menu state, form interactions, accessibility-friendly controls, security checks, and the ability to remember that you have seen the policy confirmation card.

4. Preference storage

The site may remember non-sensitive choices, such as whether the policy notice has been accepted. This keeps the interface clean when you return and avoids showing the same notice on every page view.

5. Third-party tools

The website may load third-party resources such as fonts, animation libraries, slider libraries, maps, hosting scripts, email links, or form processing services. These tools can set or read their own cookies depending on your browser and the provider settings.

6. Analytics and advertising

If analytics or advertising tools are added, they may help measure page visits, campaign performance, or user interaction patterns. Any non-essential tracking should be reviewed and adjusted in this policy when those tools are introduced.

7. Managing cookies

Most browsers allow you to delete cookies, block cookies, limit third-party cookies, or clear local storage. Blocking essential storage may affect interactive behavior, but the main website content should remain available.

8. Retention

Browser storage can last for a single session or remain until it expires, is replaced, or is deleted by you. Server-side technical logs may be retained for security, maintenance, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this Cookie Policy when the platform or its technology changes. Questions can be sent to hello@roofly.network.

Cookie Policy

How Roofly uses small browser files and similar technologies.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by a website in your browser. Similar technologies can help remember preferences, understand basic usage, or support site functionality.

2. How this site uses them

Roofly may use essential browser storage to support navigation, the mobile menu, accessibility preferences, and form behavior. The interface also loads AOS and Swiper libraries from a content delivery network for animations and sliders.

3. Your choices

Most browsers let you clear or block cookies in their settings. Blocking essential storage may affect some interactive behavior, but you can still read the main content of the site.

4. Changes

We may update this policy when the platform or its technology changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

5. Contact

Questions can be sent to hello@roofly.network.

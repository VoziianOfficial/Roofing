Privacy Policy

How Roofly handles information shared through this platform.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Roofly Network handles information collected through this website. Roofly is an independent discovery platform that helps users find and connect with roofing providers. Roofly does not perform roofing services.

2. Information you choose to share

If you use our contact form, we may collect your name, email address, selected service category, message, and privacy consent. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and help route it toward a relevant provider conversation.

3. How information is used

We use submitted information to operate the enquiry flow, communicate with you, prevent spam, improve the platform, and meet legal obligations. We do not ask for information that is not needed for an initial enquiry.

4. Sharing

We may share an enquiry with a relevant roofing provider when this is necessary to respond to the request or when you have asked to be connected. Provider conversations, estimates, and service decisions happen directly between you and the provider.

5. Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, to resolve enquiries, and to meet legal requirements. No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, but reasonable safeguards are used.

6. Your choices

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it where applicable. Email hello@roofly.network to make a request.

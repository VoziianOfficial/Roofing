1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how {legalName} handles information collected through this website. {siteName} is an independent discovery platform that helps users find and connect with roofing providers. {siteName} does not perform roofing services.

2. Information you choose to share

If you use our contact form, we may collect your name, email address, selected service category, message, and privacy consent. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and help route it toward a relevant provider conversation.

3. Information collected automatically

When you browse the site, basic technical information may be processed by the website, hosting environment, browser, or security tools. This can include device type, browser type, page URL, time of visit, referral source, and approximate diagnostic data needed to keep the site working.

4. How information is used

We use submitted information to operate the enquiry flow, communicate with you, prevent spam, improve the platform, maintain security, understand which service pages are useful, and meet legal obligations. We do not ask for information that is not needed for an initial enquiry.

5. Sharing with providers and services

We may share an enquiry with a relevant roofing provider when this is necessary to respond to the request or when you have asked to be connected. We may also use service providers for hosting, email delivery, form processing, analytics, security, and site maintenance. Provider conversations, estimates, and service decisions happen directly between you and the provider.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The site may use essential browser storage to remember preferences such as policy confirmation, support form behavior, and keep interactive features working. Non-essential tracking, advertising cookies, or analytics tools should be reviewed in the Cookie Policy when added or changed.

7. Retention

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, to resolve enquiries, maintain basic records, prevent abuse, and meet legal requirements. When information is no longer needed, we aim to delete, anonymize, or securely archive it.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the platform, but no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. You should avoid sending sensitive documents, payment information, or unrelated personal details through the initial contact form.

9. Your choices

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, ask us to delete it where applicable, or withdraw from further communications. Some records may need to be retained where required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

10. Children

This website is intended for adults looking for roofing information or provider connections. We do not knowingly collect information from children through the enquiry form.

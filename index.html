Roofing insights

Small details,
bigger roof performance

Lighter shingle colors reflect heat, keeping attics noticeably cooler through peak summer afternoons.

Balanced attic ventilation stops trapped moisture from quietly rotting the roof deck underneath.

Metal roofing is fully recyclable, making it one of the most sustainable long-term roofing choices.

Clear gutters keep water moving away from the roofline instead of pooling near the foundation.

A properly installed roof is your home's first line of defense against wind, hail, and heavy rain.

Most roof leaks start at flashing around chimneys and vents, not through the shingles themselves.

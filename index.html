Good to know

Questions before
you connect.

Roofly helps you find and compare providers. The provider you choose is responsible for discussing scope, pricing, timelines, and suitability with you directly.

Roofly is an independent discovery platform that helps users explore roofing needs and connect with relevant roofing providers. It is not a roofing contractor and does not perform roofing services.

We help organize the search around your needs. Any provider information or match should be reviewed by you, and your final decision remains your own.

Ask about relevant experience, materials, scope, timing, written estimates, warranties, insurance, and how changes are handled. A good provider should welcome clear questions.

Send a short enquiry through the form below. Include the kind of property, the roofing need, and anything you already know about the project.
